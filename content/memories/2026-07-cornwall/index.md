We went down on the Friday and it rained for two solid days. The cottage had a
wood burner and a shelf of paperbacks with the covers falling off, so this was
not the disaster it sounds like.

## The one good afternoon

Sunday cleared up about eleven. We walked the cliff path east, ate chips
sitting on a wall, and I got knocked over by a wave I had definitely seen
coming.

Things worth remembering:

- The bakery in the village opens at seven and sells out by nine.
- Take proper boots. The path is mud from about half a mile in.
- The tide comes in faster than it looks.

> Never turn your back on the sea.
>
> — everyone, repeatedly, before it happened
