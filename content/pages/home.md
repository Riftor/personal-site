I'm finishing a B.S. in Data Science at Purdue (December 2026, with an economics minor).
This past summer I was at Archer Aviation building telemetry for the internal tooling
their engineers use every day; before that I led the business side of a 200-member
aerial robotics team and spent a couple of summers turning messy user data into
something worth looking at.

Off the clock I build for a smaller audience: a modded game server for my friends, a
voice assistant that reads my calendar so I don't have to, this site.

Here's how this place works: [work](/work) is the record — internships, research, and
projects, in enough depth to mean something. [About](/about) is the longer story. And if
you know me, there's a private half — photos, memories, what I'm up to — that opens when
you sign in.
